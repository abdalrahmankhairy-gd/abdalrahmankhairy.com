A curated selection of logo and identity marks, created between 2020 and 2026.

The focus here is not quantity, but intention. The story below follows the four steps I use on every brand.

01 · Uncover

Many industries, many places.

The 2025 selection alone spans technology, AI, healthcare, medical marketing, wellness, skincare, logistics, charity initiatives and personal brands, for clients and organizations across Saudi Arabia, the United Kingdom, Egypt, the United Arab Emirates, Syria and Palestine.

Every mark begins the way the larger projects do, by understanding what the brand stands for before drawing anything.

02 · Define

Strategy first, then the mark.

Each mark was developed at the intersection of strategy, branding and storytelling.

03 · Narrate

Meaning that lasts.

The marks are designed to function beyond aesthetics: as a clear, adaptable system that carries meaning, direction and long-term relevance.

04 · Express

Marks in Arabic, English and both.
