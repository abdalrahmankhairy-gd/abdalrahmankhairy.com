How I craft stories

Four steps, from what is true to what is seen.

  1. 01

    Uncover

    Conversations with the founder, the team and the market, until the real story of the business is clear.

  2. 02

    Define

    Positioning, audience and the one idea the brand will own, written down and agreed.

  3. 03

    Narrate

    The brand story, personality and voice, so the brand sounds like itself everywhere.

  4. 04

    Express

    Identity and packaging designed from the strategy, then handed over with clear guidelines.
